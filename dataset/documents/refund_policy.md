# Refund Policy

**Company:** NovaForge Enterprise Solutions  
**Document ID:** POL-REF-001  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Updated:** November 1, 2026  
**Owner:** Finance & Customer Experience

---

## 1. Purpose

This Refund Policy explains when customers may request a refund, how refunds are processed, and which order statuses and product categories are eligible.

## 2. Eligibility Window

- Standard physical products: refund requests must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of the delivery date.
- Software licenses and digital products: refund requests must be submitted within **14 calendar days** of purchase, and only if the license has not been activated.
- Defective or incorrect items: may be refunded or replaced outside the standard window when supported by photographic evidence or a support ticket.

## 3. Order Status Rules

| Order Status | Refund Eligible? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| pending | Yes | Cancel and reverse authorization |
| confirmed | Yes | Cancel before warehouse pick |
| processing | Conditional | Allowed only before shipping label creation |
| shipped | No (use return) | Customer must refuse delivery or open a return |
| delivered | Yes (within window) | Subject to inspection |
| cancelled | N/A | No further refund action |
| returned | Yes | Credit after warehouse QC |
| overdue | Conditional | Escalated to Operations Manager |

## 4. Refund Methods

1. Original payment method is preferred for completed payments.
2. Bank transfers may take **5–10 business days**.
3. Card refunds typically appear in **3–5 business days**.
4. Invoice / net-30 accounts receive a credit memo applied to the next billing cycle.
5. Partially refunded payments are allowed when only some line items are returned.

## 5. Non-Refundable Items

- Custom-configured hardware built to customer specification
- Opened consumables and sealed software media that has been unsealed
- Services already rendered (on-site setup, training sessions)
- Clearance items marked “final sale”

## 6. Partial Refunds

When an order contains multiple line items, refunds are calculated as:

`line_total + proportional tax − restocking fee (if applicable)`

Shipping is refunded only when the entire order is returned, or when the return is caused by a NovaForge error.

## 7. Restocking Fee

A restocking fee of **15%** may apply to opened but unused products returned without defect. No restocking fee applies for defective, damaged, or incorrectly shipped items.

## 8. How to Request a Refund

1. Open a support ticket with category `refund_request`.
2. Include the order number, product SKUs, and reason.
3. Wait for an agent to approve or request more information.
4. For delivered goods, obtain a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) before shipping items back.

## 9. Escalation

Urgent refund disputes above **$2,500** must be escalated to an Operations Manager within one business day.

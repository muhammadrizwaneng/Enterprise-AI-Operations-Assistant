# Customer Support Guidelines

**Company:** NovaForge Enterprise Solutions  
**Document ID:** SUP-GDL-005  
**Effective Date:** April 1, 2025  
**Last Updated:** November 5, 2026  
**Owner:** Customer Support Leadership

---

## 1. Mission

Provide accurate, empathetic, and timely support for billing, shipping, product defects, refunds, and account questions while protecting company and customer data.

## 2. Ticket Categories

| Category | Typical Issues | Default Priority |
|---|---|---|
| billing | invoice disputes, double charges | medium |
| shipping | delays, wrong address, tracking | medium |
| product_defect | DOA, failure under warranty | high |
| refund_request | return/refund eligibility | medium |
| account | login, profile, company admin | medium |
| general_inquiry | product advice, policy questions | low |

Promote a ticket to **urgent** when the customer faces business downtime, a security concern, or a dispute above **$2,500**.

## 3. Priority SLA Targets

| Priority | First Response | Resolution Target |
|---|---|---|
| low | 8 business hours | 5 business days |
| medium | 4 business hours | 3 business days |
| high | 2 business hours | 1 business day |
| urgent | 30 minutes | Same business day |

## 4. Required Ticket Fields

Every ticket must include:

- Valid customer reference
- Clear subject line
- Detailed description
- Status and priority
- Category
- Optional linked order number when the issue is order-related
- Assigned support agent (except during intake queue)

## 5. Status Workflow

1. **open** — newly created, not yet triaged  
2. **in_progress** — agent actively working  
3. **waiting_customer** — blocked on customer reply or documents  
4. **resolved** — solution provided; awaiting confirmation window  
5. **closed** — confirmed complete or auto-closed after no response

Do not skip from `open` directly to `closed` without a resolution note.

## 6. Order-Linked Investigations

When a ticket references an order:

1. Verify the order belongs to the customer.
2. Check order status, shipment dates, and payment status.
3. Confirm line-item totals match the order subtotal.
4. Apply Refund Policy and Delivery Policy before promising outcomes.
5. Record RMA numbers for physical returns.

## 7. Communication Standards

- Use plain language; avoid internal jargon.
- Never blame the customer.
- Provide concrete next steps and timelines.
- Summarize policy citations when denying a request.
- Offer alternatives (replacement, store credit, partial refund) when full refunds are not allowed.

## 8. Escalation Path

1. Support Agent  
2. Senior Support / Queue Lead  
3. Operations Manager (fulfillment & overdue)  
4. Finance (refunds above policy thresholds)  
5. Admin / Security (account compromise)

## 9. Quality Checklist Before Closing

- [ ] Customer question answered
- [ ] Order/payment actions completed in system
- [ ] Knowledge article linked when applicable
- [ ] Follow-up date cleared or scheduled
- [ ] No unredacted secrets left in notes

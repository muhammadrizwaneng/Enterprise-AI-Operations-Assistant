## 1. Welcome

This handbook introduces company culture, roles, conduct expectations, and operational guidelines for all NovaForge employees.

## 2. Employment Roles

NovaForge uses three primary system roles:

1. **Admin** — system configuration, user provisioning, and security oversight.
2. **Support Agent** — customer ticket handling, refund triage, and order lookups.
3. **Operations Manager** — fulfillment oversight, overdue order escalation, and KPI review.

Employees must only use systems and data appropriate to their assigned role.

## 3. Working Hours & Availability

- Core collaboration hours: **10:00–16:00** local office time, Monday–Friday.
- Support agents on rotating shifts cover **08:00–20:00**.
- Overtime for fulfillment peaks must be pre-approved by an Operations Manager.

## 4. Code of Conduct

Employees must:

- Treat customers and colleagues with respect.
- Protect confidential customer and payment data.
- Never share account credentials.
- Disclose conflicts of interest to HR promptly.
- Avoid discriminatory language and harassment of any kind.

Violations may result in coaching, written warning, or termination depending on severity.

## 5. Customer Data Handling

- Access customer records only when required for an assigned ticket or order.
- Do not export customer lists to personal devices.
- Use approved tools for screenshots and diagnostics.
- Report suspected data leaks to IT Security within **1 hour**.

## 6. Ticket Handling Expectations

Support agents should:

1. Acknowledge new tickets within **4 business hours**.
2. Set priority accurately (`low`, `medium`, `high`, `urgent`).
3. Escalate urgent billing or security issues the same day.
4. Document every customer commitment in the ticket thread.
5. Close tickets only after customer confirmation or documented no-response after **5 business days**.

## 7. Time Off

- Submit PTO requests at least **7 days** in advance when possible.
- Emergency leave should be reported to the direct manager as soon as practical.
- Support coverage must be confirmed before multi-day leave is approved.

## 8. Equipment & Remote Work

Company laptops remain NovaForge property. Remote employees must use VPN for production systems and keep OS patches current within **14 days** of release.

## 9. Acknowledgment

All employees acknowledge that they have read this handbook during onboarding and after each major revision.

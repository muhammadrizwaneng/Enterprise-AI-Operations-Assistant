# Delivery Policy

**Company:** NovaForge Enterprise Solutions  
**Document ID:** POL-DEL-002  
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2025  
**Last Updated:** October 15, 2026  
**Owner:** Operations & Fulfillment

---

## 1. Overview

NovaForge ships business hardware, peripherals, and licensed software to customers worldwide. This policy defines shipping methods, timelines, free-shipping thresholds, and exception handling for delayed or overdue orders.

## 2. Processing Times

- **In-stock items:** warehouse processing within **1–2 business days** after payment confirmation.
- **Back-ordered items:** customer receives an estimated ship date within **24 hours** of order confirmation.
- Orders placed after **15:00 local warehouse time** are processed the next business day.

## 3. Shipping Methods

| Method | Typical Transit | Use Case |
|---|---|---|
| Standard Ground | 3–7 business days | Default domestic |
| Expedited | 2–3 business days | Priority business orders |
| Overnight | Next business day | Urgent replacements |
| Freight | Scheduled | Large furniture / bulk |

## 4. Free Shipping Threshold

Orders with a merchandise subtotal of **$150.00 or more** qualify for free standard ground shipping within the contiguous service area. Expedited and overnight upgrades remain chargeable.

Standard flat shipping for below-threshold orders is **$9.99**.

## 5. Delivery Status Definitions

- **pending / confirmed:** order accepted, not yet picked
- **processing:** warehouse is picking and packing
- **shipped:** carrier scan exists; tracking shared with customer
- **delivered:** carrier confirmation of delivery
- **overdue:** expected delivery date passed with no delivery scan
- **returned:** package returned to warehouse or customer-initiated return received
- **cancelled:** fulfillment stopped before shipment

## 6. Overdue Orders

An order becomes **overdue** when:

1. The promised delivery date has passed, and
2. No delivery confirmation exists, and
3. The order is still marked shipped or processing.

Support agents must:

1. Verify carrier tracking.
2. Contact the carrier within one business day.
3. Offer a replacement or refund if the package is confirmed lost after **7 calendar days**.

## 7. Address Changes

Address changes are allowed only before the shipping label is created. After shipment, customers must coordinate with the carrier or refuse delivery and reorder.

## 8. Signature & Restricted Delivery

Orders above **$1,000.00** require adult signature confirmation unless the customer opts out in writing. High-risk destinations may require freight appointment delivery.

## 9. Damaged in Transit

Customers must report visible damage within **48 hours** of delivery with photos of packaging and product. NovaForge will arrange a replacement or refund under the Refund Policy.

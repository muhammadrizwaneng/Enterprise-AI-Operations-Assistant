## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how NovaForge collects, uses, stores, and shares personal data related to customers, website visitors, and support interactions.

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Customer Account Data
- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Company name
- Billing and shipping address
- Account activity timestamps

### 2.2 Order & Payment Data
- Order numbers and line items
- Payment references, method type, and status
- Transaction amounts and timestamps  
NovaForge does **not** store full payment card numbers in application databases.

### 2.3 Support Data
- Ticket content, attachments, and correspondence
- Agent assignment history
- Priority and resolution metadata

### 2.4 Technical Data
- IP address, browser type, and device identifiers for security monitoring
- Application logs needed for fraud prevention and reliability

## 3. How We Use Data

We use personal data to:

1. Fulfill and deliver orders
2. Process payments and refunds
3. Provide customer support
4. Detect fraud and secure accounts
5. Improve product catalog and operations
6. Meet legal and audit obligations

## 4. Legal Bases

Depending on jurisdiction, processing may rely on contract performance, legitimate interests (security and service improvement), legal obligation, or consent where required.

## 5. Data Sharing

We share data only with:

- Payment processors and banks
- Shipping carriers and freight partners
- Cloud infrastructure and email providers under contract
- Regulators when legally required

We do **not** sell personal data.

## 6. Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Customer profile | Active relationship + 7 years |
| Orders & invoices | 7 years |
| Support tickets | 3 years after closure |
| Security logs | 12 months |
| Marketing preferences | Until withdrawal of consent |

## 7. Security Controls

- Role-based access (admin, support agent, operations manager)
- Encryption in transit (TLS)
- Least-privilege database credentials
- Audit logging for sensitive record access
- Periodic access reviews

## 8. Customer Rights

Subject to applicable law, customers may request:

- Access to their personal data
- Correction of inaccurate data
- Deletion where legally permitted
- Restriction or objection to certain processing
- Data portability for account and order records

Requests should be submitted via a support ticket with category `account` or by emailing privacy@novaforge.example.

## 9. International Transfers

Where data is transferred across borders, NovaForge uses contractual safeguards and vendor assessments to protect personal information.

## 10. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@novaforge.example
